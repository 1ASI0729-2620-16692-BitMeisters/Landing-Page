Before you start

Fewer questions.
More clarity.

What type of operation is it designed for?

FleetSafe is designed for freight transport companies that need to centralize pre-trip inspections, assess vehicle conditions, and manage issues before each operation.

What determines whether a vehicle is cleared?

Inspection results are checked against the company’s assessment rules. Based on these conditions, a vehicle is classified as cleared, flagged, or not cleared. The supervisor can review the result and manage reported issues.

Can drivers use their phones?

The forms are designed for mobile devices: drivers can record inspections, add observations, and attach photo evidence from their own device.

Does this demo store my company’s data?

No. This page includes a demo with fictional information to illustrate the FleetSafe workflow. Filters and inspections run in your browser; they do not create accounts or send information.
