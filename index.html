Safety & Preventive Control

Ensure Your Fleet is Ready to Operate Safely

A specialized web platform for cargo transport companies. We centralize preventive control so you can easily evaluate vehicle conditions, identify risks, and authorize operations before they start.

Start Inspection Learn More

Moving Away from Scattered Processes

Transport companies struggle to centrally manage security conditions. Relying on physical formats and spreadsheets makes it difficult to quickly identify risks, expired documents, and pending incidents.

Platform Core Capabilities

1

Digital Pre-Operational Inspections

Drivers can digitally record the status of vehicle components, safety elements, and documentation right from their devices.

2

Rule-Based Evaluation

The system automatically evaluates inspection results against company-established rules to quickly identify risks and problems.

3

Operational Authorization

Instantly determine the preventive condition of a vehicle. The system classifies units as Enabled, Observed, or Not Enabled to operate.

4

Incident Management

Centralize problems detected during inspections. Record evidence, track corrective actions, and maintain a historical record.

Designed for Your Entire Operation

For Administrators

Configure evaluation rules, manage users, and maintain an organized record of the preventive state of the entire fleet.

For Fleet Supervisors

Review inspection results, manage incidents, and easily verify which units are authorized to safely operate.

For Drivers

Execute quick digital inspections on assigned vehicles and easily attach evidence or observations when problems are found.
